Our Story

A Love That Always Finds Its Way Home

How It Began

Some love stories begin with a chance meeting. Ours began with someone who believed in us before we ever believed in ourselves.

At the time, Thomas's father, Brian, was my boss. He knew me well, and he often spoke about his eldest son. Deep in his heart, he hoped we would meet, convinced that we might be the right match for each other.

When I first met Thomas, I noticed how shy he was. He wasn't the loudest person in the room, but there was something quietly captivating about him. He had a warm smile, kind eyes, and carried himself with a maturity well beyond his years. More than anything, I was drawn to his kindness, sincerity, and gentle nature.

In the beginning, we agreed to give our relationship a chance because we both wanted to make Brian happy. Neither of us knew that what started as a simple introduction would become the greatest love story of our lives.

As the days turned into weeks, we found ourselves talking for hours. Late-night conversations became our favourite part of the day as we shared our dreams, fears, ambitions, and hopes for the future. Little by little, two strangers became best friends.

Without even realising it, we had become home to one another.

Before long, I discovered that no moment of happiness felt complete unless I could share it with Thomas. He had become the most important person in my life, and I couldn't imagine my future without him.

Tested by Distance

Like every great love story, ours was tested.

For three long years we lived apart, separated by distance but never by heart. Those years were filled with longing, uncertainty, and countless goodbyes. We missed birthdays, celebrations, and ordinary moments that most couples take for granted. There were lonely nights, difficult days, and moments when all we wanted was to be together.

Yet those years taught us something priceless.

We learned that love isn't measured by how often you're together, but by how faithfully you continue choosing each other when being together isn't possible.

Every phone call, every message, every visit, and every goodbye strengthened the foundation of our relationship. Instead of pulling us apart, the distance brought us closer.

When we decided to marry, it wasn't because life would be easy. It was because we had already weathered life's storms and discovered that every challenge only made our love stronger.

Building Our Life Together

When I moved to Australia, we finally began building our life together as husband and wife.

Life wasn't always perfect. Thomas's work often demanded long hours, but he always found ways to make our time together special. Whether it was a beautiful dinner, a weekend away, or simply exploring Sydney together, he made ordinary moments feel extraordinary. Those memories became treasures that still make us smile today.

Eight months later, we moved interstate to Adelaide, South Australia, ready to begin another chapter of our lives.

Not long after, we faced one of the hardest seasons of our marriage.

Thomas was deployed to Afghanistan.

For nine months I lived with fear, uncertainty, and constant prayer. Every day I waited for news, hoping and believing that he would return home safely. It was one of the greatest sacrifices we have ever made, and although those months were incredibly difficult, they changed us forever.

When he returned home, we appreciated each other in ways we never had before. We realised that every ordinary day together was a gift.

From there, our dreams slowly became reality.

One of our proudest achievements was building our home. Together we chose every detail—the design, the colours, the finishes, and the little touches that reflected who we were as a couple. It became so much more than bricks and mortar. It became the place where our dreams lived, where laughter echoed through the rooms, and where our family would grow.

Along the way we celebrated many other milestones. We bought our cars together, including our beloved first Jeep that still brings back wonderful memories whenever we think about it.

Our Miracle

Then came one of the greatest blessings God could ever have given us.

Our IVF journey.

It wasn't an easy path, but through faith, hope, and God's grace, we welcomed our beautiful daughter, Isabella, into our lives. The day she was born, our hearts expanded in ways we never thought possible. She became the greatest reminder that miracles often come after the hardest journeys.

Our marriage has continued to experience seasons of togetherness and seasons of distance.

Because of Thomas's work, long-distance has remained part of our story. Our love has often been written in quiet moments—the tears as the garage door closes behind his car, the hugs that last just a little longer at airport terminals, the lonely evenings, and the overwhelming joy of finally welcoming each other home again.

Every goodbye has hurt.

But every goodbye has also carried the promise that we would find our way back to each other.

Adventures Together

Whenever life has allowed, we've made it a priority to create memories together. We've escaped to beautiful hotels, explored new places around Australia, and travelled overseas, reminding ourselves that time together is life's greatest luxury.

Among all our adventures, one journey will always hold a special place in our hearts.

Italy.

We floated through Venice on a romantic gondola, tossed our wishes into the Trevi Fountain, wandered hand in hand through ancient streets, and experienced the incredible privilege of attending the final Mass celebrated by Pope Benedict XVI at St. Peter's Basilica. Those moments became part of the beautiful collection of memories that define our marriage.

Renewing Our Vows

Today, after seventeen years together, we stand here to renew the vows we made all those years ago.

Not because our promises have faded, but because they have endured.

We renew them to celebrate the love that has carried us through every challenge, every season, every goodbye, every homecoming, every victory, and every unexpected blessing.

Our journey has taught us that real love isn't found only in grand romantic gestures.

It's found in patience when life is difficult.

In faith when the future is uncertain.

In forgiveness when we make mistakes.

In sacrifice when one gives more than the other.

In laughter during ordinary days.

And most importantly, in the daily decision to keep choosing each other, over and over again.

Looking Forward

As we look toward the future, we dream of more adventures, more travels, more sunsets, more laughter, and more time together. We want to keep creating memories, strengthening our marriage, and embracing every opportunity life gives us.

One day, when we're old and sitting together in a nursing home, I hope we'll look through our photographs—the holidays, the birthdays, the travels, the family celebrations, and the ordinary everyday moments—and smile.

Not because our life was perfect.

But because we lived it together.

Our story began because one man believed we belonged together.

It grew through late-night conversations, years of long distance, sacrifice, unwavering faith, marriage, a home built with love, the miracle of our daughter, and countless moments that have shaped who we are today.

Seventeen years later, our story is still unfolding.

My Promise

Thomas, you are my safe place, my greatest blessing, my best friend, and the love of my life.

Through every distance, every challenge, every goodbye, every reunion, every joy, and every season, I would choose you again.

And I will keep choosing you—for all the days God gives us.

Today, with a grateful heart, I renew my vows to you.

I promise to continue loving you, standing beside you, encouraging you, praying for you, laughing with you, and building our future together.

Because no matter where life leads us...

My heart will always find its way home to you.
